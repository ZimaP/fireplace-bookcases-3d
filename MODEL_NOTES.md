# Model notes and drawing mapping

This project was modeled from the two supplied images only. It intentionally does not import code, geometry, dimensions, or assets from any earlier cabinet/bookcase project.

## Details carried into the parametric model

- Symmetrical left and right built-in bookcases flanking a central fireplace/chimney projection.
- Two upper bays per bookcase with a fixed 1-1/2 inch center divider and 1-1/2 inch front frame logic.
- Adjustable shelf system with visible 5 mm pin holes in front and rear rows, using typical two-holes-up / two-holes-down adjustment logic.
- Shelf span checks from the drawing:
  - 1 inch MDF: 27 inch maximum clear width.
  - 1-1/4 inch MDF: 31 inch maximum clear width.
  - 1-1/2 inch MDF: 36 inch maximum clear width.
- Field-fit side fillers/scribes and a field-adjustable crown/top-filler zone.
- Four inset Shaker doors per lower cabinet, recessed toe kick, adjustable feet, face frame, countertop/transition top, lower interior shelf, and finished back panels.
- Classical center mantel with layered shelf/crown, pilasters, fluting, rosettes, dentils, decorative frieze, herringbone firebox interior, stone hearth, metal insert frame, glass, ceramic logs, ember bed, and procedural animated flame.
- U-shaped room shell, side walls, central chimney breast, floor, baseboards, and optional ceiling, based on the room-layout image.

## Starting dimensions

The source screenshot does not show complete overall room, bookcase, fireplace, or mantel dimensions. The app therefore starts with proportional assumptions that reproduce the supplied layout and remain editable. Construction thicknesses are independent parameters and do not automatically scale when overall width, height, or room dimensions change.

For fabrication accuracy, replace the starting assumptions with verified field dimensions or the original full-size CAD/PDF drawing.

## Desktop-only scope

The interface is intentionally designed for a wide desktop viewport. There is no separate mobile UI or mobile model.
